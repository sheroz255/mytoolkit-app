About MyToolkit

MyToolkit is a free collection of simple, fast online tools for everyday digital tasks — text tools, PDF utilities, image tools, developer utilities, SEO helpers and more. Every tool is designed to be quick to use, easy to understand, and free of unnecessary clutter.

Our Approach

Most of the tools on MyToolkit run entirely in your browser. That means your text, files and data are processed on your own device and are not uploaded to a server unless a tool specifically requires it (in which case that is stated clearly on the tool's page).

Why MyToolkit

  • Free to use, with no sign-up required
  • Fast, lightweight tools that work on any device
  • Clear, honest descriptions of what each tool actually does
  • No unnecessary complexity — just the tool you need
