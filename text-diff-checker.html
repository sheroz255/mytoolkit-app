Free Text Diff Checker

Compare two blocks of text and instantly see exactly what was added, removed or changed, either line-by-line or word-by-word. Everything runs in your browser — nothing you paste is ever uploaded.

How This Diff Checker Works

This tool compares your two texts using a longest-common-subsequence algorithm, the same approach used by standard diff tools. Line-by-Line mode compares whole lines, useful for comparing documents, code, or paragraphs. Word-by-Word mode compares individual words, useful for spotting small wording changes within the same sentence. Removed content is shown with a red strikethrough, added content with a green highlight, and unchanged parts stay plain.

Features

Line & Word Modes

Compare whole lines for documents/code, or individual words for wording changes.

Clear Highlighting

Additions in green, removals in red strikethrough — easy to scan at a glance.

Change Summary

See counts of added, removed and unchanged lines or words instantly.

100% Private

Both texts stay in your browser — nothing is uploaded or stored.
