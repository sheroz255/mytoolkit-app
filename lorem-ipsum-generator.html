What Is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is scrambled, pseudo-Latin placeholder text that has been the industry standard for filling design mockups and layouts since the 1500s. Because it reads like natural text without any actual meaning, it lets designers and developers focus on layout, typography and spacing without the distraction of real content. This generator lets you control exactly how much text you need — a few words for a button, a couple of sentences for a caption, or several paragraphs for a full page mockup.
