Free Text Reverser

Reverse your text instantly — flip the whole string backwards, reverse word order, reverse the letters inside each word, or reverse the order of lines. Works correctly with emojis and non-English text, right in your browser.

How Each Mode Works

Reverse Characters flips your entire text backwards, letter by letter (so "Hello" becomes "olleH"). Reverse Word Order keeps each word spelled normally but reverses the order they appear in (so "Hello there friend" becomes "friend there Hello"). Reverse Each Word keeps word order the same but flips the letters within every individual word. Reverse Lines keeps each line's text intact but reverses the order the lines appear in, useful for multi-line lists or paragraphs. This tool correctly handles emojis and multi-byte Unicode characters, so reversing text with emojis won't corrupt them into broken symbols.
