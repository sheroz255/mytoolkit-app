How to Compress PDF Files Online for Free

Large PDF files can be frustrating when attempting to attach them to emails, upload them to job portals, or submit them through government applications with strict file size limits. The MyToolkit Compress PDF tool offers an instant, browser-based solution that shrinks document file sizes while retaining optimal readability.

  1. Select your PDF document: Drag and drop your file into the designated upload area or click to select from your device.
  2. Choose compression intensity: Pick between Extreme Compression (maximum reduction), Recommended (balanced), or Less Compression (highest image quality).
  3. Click "Compress PDF Now": Your document will process instantly in your browser and provide a direct download link alongside real file size reduction metrics.

Why Use Our Client-Side PDF Compressor?

Understanding PDF Compression Levels

Extreme Compression

Re-samples images to lower DPI and higher JPEG compression ratio. Ideal for reducing huge scanned manuals or multi-megabyte PDFs to under 2MB for email limits.

Recommended

Balances crisp text legibility and clean images with significant size savings. Recommended for resumes, invoices, and office documents.

Less Compression

Applies gentle optimization while maintaining high resolution. Perfect for portfolios, presentations, and graphic design documents.
