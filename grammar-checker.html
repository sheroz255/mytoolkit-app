Free Grammar Checker

Scan your text for common, mechanically detectable writing issues — double spaces, repeated words, commonly confused words, common misspellings, punctuation spacing and capitalization — instantly, right in your browser.

How this differs from AI grammar tools: Full AI-powered grammar checking (like Grammarly) requires a paid server-side language model. This tool runs entirely in your browser with rule-based checks, so it catches clear, mechanical issues reliably, but it won't catch every subtle grammar or style mistake a human editor or AI service might.

What This Tool Checks

This checker looks for issues that can be reliably identified through pattern matching: double spaces between words, repeated words ("the the"), commonly confused words (their/there/they're, its/it's, your/you're, then/than, affect/effect, loose/lose), a list of common misspellings (definately, recieve, occured, seperate, and similar), missing spaces after punctuation, multiple punctuation marks in a row, and sentences not starting with a capital letter. It flags these clearly so you can review and fix them yourself — it does not automatically rewrite your text.
