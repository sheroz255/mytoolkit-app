How to Split PDF Files Online for Free

Splitting a PDF document into smaller parts or isolating individual pages is one of the most essential daily digital tasks. Whether you need to extract a single receipt from a monthly statement, share a specific chapter from a textbook, or separate mixed document scans, the MyToolkit Split PDF tool delivers a simple, lightning-fast, and completely secure solution.

  1. Upload your file: Drag and drop your PDF into the upload area above or click to select a file from your computer or mobile device.
  2. Choose your splitting method: Select custom page ranges (e.g. 1-4, 7), extract every page individually, visually click thumbnail previews, or divide into equal chunks.
  3. Download your extracted PDF: Click Split PDF Now to generate and download your new PDF file or ZIP package instantly.

100% Client-Side Privacy & Security

Unlike traditional online converters that upload your personal documents to remote cloud servers, MyToolkit runs on a pure browser-side architecture. Using WebAssembly and HTML5 Web APIs, all processing happens locally inside your device's web browser memory.

  • Complete Confidentiality: Your files are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted over any network server.
  • Zero Wait Times: Instant execution without uploading heavy files over slow internet connections.
  • No Usage Limits: Free forever with no file limits, no registration, no software installation, and no added watermarks.
  • Cross-Platform Compatibility: Works seamlessly across Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS (iPhone/iPad), and Android devices.
