How the Free MyToolkit Word Counter Works

The MyToolkit Word Counter is a precision online text analyzer crafted specifically for content writers, editors, students, copywriters, and search engine optimization specialists. Whether you are drafting a university thesis, tuning Google ad copy, drafting social media captions, or preparing a speech, knowing exact word and character counts is essential.

Unlike rudimentary counter scripts that simply split by basic spaces, our tool uses high-precision Unicode-aware boundary detection. It accurately detects apostrophes, hyphenated phrases, contractions, multiple punctuation marks, and multi-byte emojis without generating false counts or broken metrics.

Understanding the Flesch Reading Ease Formula

The Flesch Reading Ease index is one of the most widely recognized readability tests. It is mathematically defined as:

Score = 206.835 - (1.015 × ASL) - (84.6 × ASW)
Where ASL = Average Sentence Length (Words / Sentences) and ASW = Average Syllables per Word (Syllables / Words).

A score of 60 to 70 is considered plain English, easily understood by 13- to 15-year-old students. Web copywriters aim for scores between 60 and 80 to maximize retention and conversion rates across diverse audiences.
