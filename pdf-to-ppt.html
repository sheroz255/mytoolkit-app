Free PDF to PPT Converter

Convert your PDF into a PowerPoint presentation instantly. Each page of your PDF becomes one slide, ready to open, present, or edit further in PowerPoint.

How this works: Each PDF page is rendered as a high-quality image and placed on its own slide. This preserves the exact visual layout of your PDF, but the text on each slide is part of the image (not separately editable PowerPoint text), since reliably extracting editable text and layout from any PDF requires processing this free browser-only tool doesn't perform.

How This Converter Works

This tool reads your PDF directly in your browser, renders each page at high resolution, and places that page image onto its own slide in a new PowerPoint file, matching each page's original aspect ratio. The result is a genuine, valid .pptx file you can open in PowerPoint, Google Slides, or Keynote, present directly, or use as a starting point — you can still add new editable text boxes, notes, or annotations on top of each slide. Because everything happens locally, your PDF is never uploaded to a server.
