How Reading Time Is Calculated

Reading time is estimated by dividing the total word count by a chosen reading speed, measured in words per minute (wpm). The average adult reads around 200-250 words per minute for normal prose, though this varies by content difficulty, familiarity, and individual reading ability. This calculator lets you choose a preset speed or enter your own, so the estimate can match your specific audience — useful for setting realistic expectations on blog posts, articles or documentation.
