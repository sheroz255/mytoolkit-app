What Each Option Does

Strip HTML tags removes markup like <p> or <div> while keeping the visible text. Remove URLs and Remove email addresses delete web links and email addresses respectively. Convert curly quotes/dashes replaces "smart" typographic quotes and em/en dashes (often introduced by word processors) with plain straight equivalents. Remove emojis strips emoji characters while keeping regular text intact. Remove numbers and Remove punctuation strip digits or punctuation marks respectively. Collapse extra whitespace reduces repeated spaces and blank lines down to a single space or line break.
