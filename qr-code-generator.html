How This QR Code Generator Works

Pick a content type — a website link, WiFi network, contact card, email, text message, phone number, WhatsApp chat, calendar event, map location, or social profile — and fill in the relevant fields. As you type, the QR code updates instantly using your chosen colors, dot style and error-correction level. When you're happy with it, download it as a PNG image or copy the raw content it encodes. Every QR code is generated locally in your browser using the standard QR encoding algorithm, so nothing you enter is ever sent anywhere.
