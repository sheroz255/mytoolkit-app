Free PDF Page Extractor

Upload a PDF, preview every page, and pick exactly the ones you need — by clicking thumbnails or typing a page range. Download your selection as a brand-new PDF. Everything happens locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded to a server.

How It Works

After you upload a PDF, this tool renders a thumbnail preview of every page directly in your browser using PDF.js. Click any page to select it — a checkmark appears in the corner — or type a page range like 1-3,5,8-10 into the range box and click "Apply Range" to select many pages at once. When you're happy with your selection, click "Extract Selected Pages" and a brand-new PDF containing only those pages, in their original order, is generated with PDF-lib and downloaded straight to your device.

Why Extract Pages From a PDF?

Share only what's needed

Pull a single contract clause, one chapter, or a few invoice pages out of a long document instead of sending the whole file.

Smaller, focused files

A 2-page extract is far easier to email, print, or upload than a 200-page original.

Keep quality intact

Pages are copied directly from the source PDF — no re-rendering, no compression, no loss of text or image quality.

100% private

Your file is processed entirely on your device. It is never uploaded to any server, so sensitive documents stay sensitive.

Common Uses

People use a PDF page extractor for all kinds of everyday tasks: pulling a single signed page out of a scanned agreement, saving just the results section of a long report, separating one chapter from an e-book or academic paper, isolating a few invoice or receipt pages for an expense claim, or grabbing a single ID/form page from a multi-page scan. Because the tool runs entirely client-side, it works just as well for short two-page files as it does for documents with hundreds of pages — the only limit is how much memory your browser has available to render thumbnails.

Tips for Best Results

If you already know exactly which pages you want, typing a page range is usually faster than clicking through thumbnails one by one — especially on a large document. You can combine ranges and single pages freely, for example 2,4-6,10. If you change your mind about a page, simply click its thumbnail again to deselect it; applying a new range does not clear your existing selection, so use "Deselect All" first if you want to start over. Very large PDFs (100+ pages) may take a few extra seconds to generate thumbnails since everything happens on your device rather than a server.
