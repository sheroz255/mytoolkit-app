Free PDF Watermark Adder

Add a custom text watermark — like "CONFIDENTIAL", "DRAFT" or your own text — to every page of a PDF. Adjust the color, size, opacity, rotation and position, preview it live, and download the result. Everything happens locally in your browser.

How It Works

After you upload a PDF, choose your watermark text and style settings on the left, and the preview on the right updates to show exactly how the first page will look — it's a real render of the actual watermarked page, not a mock-up. Pick "Diagonal Tile" to repeat the watermark across the whole page in a rotated grid, similar to how confidential documents are typically marked, or choose "Center" or a corner position for a single watermark instead. Once you're happy with the preview, click "Add Watermark & Download" to apply the same watermark to every page and save the result as a new PDF file. The original page content — text, images and formatting — is left completely untouched; the watermark is simply drawn on top.
