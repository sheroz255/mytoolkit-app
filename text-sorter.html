Free Text Sorter

Paste any list of lines and sort them A-Z, Z-A, numerically, by length, or shuffle them into random order. Optionally remove duplicates and trim whitespace, all instantly in your browser.

How Each Sort Mode Works

A → Z and Z → A sort lines alphabetically in forward or reverse order. Numeric sorts lines as numbers from smallest to largest (lines that aren't valid numbers are placed at the end). Shortest First and Longest First sort by character count. Shuffle puts your lines into a random order, useful for randomizing quiz questions, giveaway entries, or playlists.

Features

6 Sort Modes

Alphabetical, numeric, by length, or fully randomized.

Clean-Up Options

Remove duplicates, empty lines, and trim whitespace in the same step.

Case-Insensitive Option

Sort "Apple" and "apple" together, or keep case-sensitive ordering.

100% Private

Your text never leaves your browser — nothing is uploaded or stored.
