Free Palindrome Checker

Check instantly whether any word, phrase, or number reads the same forwards and backwards. By default, spaces, punctuation and capitalization are ignored, so phrases like "A man, a plan, a canal: Panama" are correctly recognized as palindromes.

How Palindrome Checking Works

A palindrome is a word, phrase or sequence that reads the same forwards and backwards. This checker normally ignores spaces, punctuation, and letter case, since phrases like "A man, a plan, a canal: Panama" or "Was it a car or a cat I saw?" are traditionally considered palindromes even though their raw text includes spaces and punctuation. If you need an exact, character-by-character check instead, enable Strict mode, which compares the text exactly as typed, including spaces, punctuation and capitalization.
