Free PDF Page Rotator

Upload a PDF, preview every page, and rotate individual pages or the whole document 90°, 180° or 270°. Everything happens locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server.

How It Works

After you upload a PDF, this tool renders a thumbnail preview of every page directly in your browser. Each page has its own rotate-left and rotate-right buttons, so you can fix pages that were scanned sideways or upside down without affecting the rest of the document. If every page needs the same treatment, use the "Rotate All Left" or "Rotate All Right" buttons to rotate the whole document at once. When you're happy with the preview, click "Download Rotated PDF" to save a new file with the rotation applied — the original page content, text and images are left completely untouched; only the page orientation flag changes.
