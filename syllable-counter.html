Free Syllable Counter

Count syllables in any word, sentence or paragraph instantly, with a per-word breakdown and running total.

How this works: English syllable counts can't be calculated with 100% accuracy from spelling alone, since pronunciation has many exceptions. This tool uses a standard vowel-group estimation method (the same approach used by many readability calculators), which is accurate for the vast majority of common words but may occasionally differ from a dictionary's exact pronunciation for unusual words.
