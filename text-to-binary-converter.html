Free Text to Binary Converter

Convert any text into binary code, or decode binary back into readable text. Correctly handles UTF-8 characters, emojis and special symbols, right in your browser.

How This Converter Works

Every character of text is stored as one or more bytes using UTF-8 encoding, the standard used across the web. This tool converts each byte of your text into its 8-digit binary representation, separated by spaces. Basic English letters and numbers use a single byte, while accented characters, symbols, and emojis may use two, three or four bytes depending on the character. Converting back reverses this exact process, decoding each 8-bit binary group back into its original UTF-8 byte sequence and reconstructing the text.
