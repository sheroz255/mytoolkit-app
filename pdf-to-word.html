How to Convert PDF to Word Online for Free

Editing text inside a PDF file can be difficult without expensive software. The MyToolkit PDF to Word converter allows you to transform static PDF documents into fully editable Microsoft Word (.docx) files or plain text within seconds directly inside your browser.

  1. Upload your PDF: Drag and drop your file into the box above or click to select a document from your computer or mobile device.
  2. Choose conversion settings: Select your preferred output format (.docx or .txt) and specify page ranges if you only need a portion of the document.
  3. Convert & Download: Click Convert to Word Document to generate your editable document instantly.

100% Client-Side Privacy & Data Security
