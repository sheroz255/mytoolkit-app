Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information MyToolkit collects and how it is used when you visit this website.

Information We Collect

MyToolkit does not require an account or sign-up, and we do not ask you to submit personal information to use our tools. We use Google Analytics to understand overall site traffic and usage patterns (such as which pages are visited and general audience location), which relies on cookies and similar technologies. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

How Our Tools Handle Your Data

Most tools on this website run entirely in your web browser. Text you type, files you select, and results generated are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. If a specific tool ever requires uploading data to complete its function, that will be stated clearly on that tool's page.

Cookies

This site uses cookies associated with Google Analytics to measure site traffic. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time; doing so may not affect the core functionality of our browser-based tools.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Analytics to measure website traffic. Google's use of data collected through Analytics is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

MyToolkit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
